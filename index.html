The woman behind it all

heya

I started this blog as a place to document my journey, and to stop being afraid of sharing it with the people around me who are just like me.

It's also where I share how my curiosity about AI has grown over time, and the positive influence I believe it's having on property.

And I'm not only curious about AI for myself. I'm curious about how it can change the property sector as a whole.
