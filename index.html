About me

The woman behind the questions

Friends call me Laizah. I started this blog because I wanted a place to be honest about what building in property really looks like, and to share how curiosity about AI is shaping the way I do it.

The Journey

I run Laiza Properties, working across development projects and building a small team of apprentices and assistants along the way. Every project has taught me something new, usually the hard way.

  • Managing renovations from first viewing to final snagging list
  • Navigating planning permission and building regulations
  • Bringing AI into day-to-day deal analysis and operations
  • Documenting the journey to my first investment property

The Person

Away from spreadsheets and site visits, I'm endlessly curious, a little bit competitive and always learning something new.

  • Believer that good questions beat fast answers
  • Happiest with a notebook, a coffee and a new idea
  • Passionate about more women building wealth through property
  • Learning in public, mistakes included
